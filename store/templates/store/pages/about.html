    What Power Pick is for

    Power Pick is an online store for electrical supplies. It is built for electricians who need the right rating and pack size without ringing round, and for homeowners who want to replace a switch, add a socket or fit a light and need to see what suits the job.

    Every listing shows its price in rupees (₹), its specifications and how many are in stock, so you can decide before you order.

    Founder

    Power Pick was founded by Janhavi Pravin Jivanwal, who set out to make buying electrical supplies as clear as reading a spec sheet: the right part, the right rating and honest stock, in one place.

    How the catalogue is organised

    Comparing ratings, pack sizes and stock

    Products of the same make that differ only in size or rating, such as a cable in several cross-sections or a board in several module counts, are grouped on one product page as options. Choose the option you need and its price and stock update with it.

    Ratings and specifications
    The specification list on each product page gives the figures that matter for that part. Check them against what your circuit and your local rules require. Power Pick cannot confirm that a product is right for your installation.
    Pack sizes
    The option label, for example a cross-section or a module count, tells you what you are buying. Compare the price of each option, not only the cheapest.
    Stock
    The stock gauge shows in stock, low, last few or out of stock. Stock is checked again at checkout, so a product that sells out while you shop is flagged before you pay.

    How ordering works

    1. Add to cart. Choose the option and quantity. You cannot add more than the stock shown.
    2. Sign in or register. You need an account to check out. Browsing does not need one.
    3. Confirm delivery details. Your address and phone number come from your profile and the checkout form.
    4. Choose how to pay. Pay online through Razorpay secure checkout, or choose cash on delivery and pay when the order arrives.
    5. Follow the order. Each order moves through Pending, Confirmed, Processing, Shipped and Delivered. You can see its status on your orders page.
