    What we store

    Power Pick keeps only what it needs to run accounts and orders.

    Your account
    Your name, username, email address and password. Passwords are stored using Django's password hashing, not as plain text, and staff cannot read them.
    Delivery details
    The phone number and delivery address in your profile, and the copy of each that is saved on every order you place.
    Order history
    Your orders: items, the price paid, quantities, status, and the payment method and status, with the Razorpay payment reference for online payments.
    Things you add
    Products you save to your wishlist and any reviews you write.

    Why we store it

    To sign you in, keep your cart, place and pay for orders, show your order and payment history, and let store staff fulfil orders. Store staff can see the orders, delivery addresses and phone numbers needed to fulfil them.

    What we never store

    Power Pick never stores card numbers, UPI details or other payment credentials. You enter them in Razorpay's own payment window, and Power Pick only receives the result and a payment reference.

    Who else sees it

    Razorpay
    Payments are handled by Razorpay. When you pay, the order amount and your payment details go to Razorpay under its own terms and privacy notice.
    Groq
    When you ask the assistant a question, the question text and a few matching product and FAQ records are sent to Groq to generate the answer. Your name, account, address and orders are not sent, but anything you type into the question is. Do not put private details in a question.

    Power Pick does not run advertising or analytics trackers. Fonts and other assets are served from the site itself.

    Cookies

    The site uses a session cookie to keep you signed in and to remember your cart, and a security cookie that protects forms from being submitted by other sites. Neither is used for tracking.

    Your choices

    You can change your name, email, phone number and delivery address from your profile at any time. Orders keep the details used when they were placed, so that a receipt stays accurate. To ask about anything else, see Contact.
