Ask the assistant

The Power Pick assistant answers questions about the catalogue, stock, accounts, ordering and payments. It works from the product listings and the store's published FAQs. It can show a signed-in customer the status of their own orders.

Questions you type are sent to Groq, an outside service, to help work out the answer. Do not put passwords, card numbers or other private details in a question. See Privacy.

What the assistant can help with

  • Finding products. Searching, browsing by category, filtering by brand or budget, and what to compare when choosing wire, MCBs, switches, lighting or tools.
  • Stock and prices. How availability is shown and why stock can change while you shop.
  • Accounts. Registering, signing in, and saving delivery details.
  • Ordering and payment. Placing an order, paying with Razorpay, what to do if payment fails, and what each order status means.

If the assistant has no verified answer, it says so rather than guessing.

Questions about an order

Open the order from your orders to see its status, items, delivery address and payment record. You can pay for or cancel an unpaid order from the same page. See Cancellations for the rules.
