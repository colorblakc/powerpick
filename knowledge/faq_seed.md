# Power Pick customer questions

## Account and sign-in

### How do I create a Power Pick account?
Open Register and enter your first name, last name, email address, a username and a password (twice). A phone number is optional. You are signed in as soon as the account is created. Public registration only creates customer accounts.

### Why does registration reject my username or email?
A username can belong to only one account, and so can an email address. If yours is already taken, sign in with it or choose a different one. The message beside the field says which one needs changing.

### Why was my new password rejected?
Read the message beside the password field. Choose a longer password that is not a common word or only numbers, and type the same password in both fields.

### How do I sign in?
Open Sign in and enter your username and password. Customers return to the page they were on, or to the shop. Staff go to the staff workspace.

### Why can't I sign in?
Check the username exactly as you registered it, and check that Caps Lock is off. Power Pick has no email password-reset page. If you still cannot get in, see the Contact page for how to reach the store.

### Do I need an account to browse products?
No. You can browse, search, read specifications and fill a cart without signing in. You need an account to check out, to see your orders and to use the wishlist.

### How do I change my name or email?
Sign in, open your profile and edit Personal details, then choose Save personal details.

### How do I save my delivery details?
Open your profile and fill in Delivery details: full name, phone, address lines, state, city and PIN code, then save. Checkout starts with those details filled in, and you can still change them for a single order.

### Does changing my profile change an order I already placed?
No. Each order keeps the name, address and phone number you used at checkout. A profile change only applies to later orders.

### How do I change my password?
Sign in, open your profile and choose Change password. Pick a password you do not use anywhere else.

### How do I sign out?
Open the account menu at the top of the page and choose Sign out. Sign in again to see your orders, wishlist or payment history.

### Can someone register as a staff member?
No. Registration only creates customer accounts. Staff access is set up separately by the store.

## Finding products

### How do I search for a product?
Use the search box at the top of every page. You can search by name, brand or rating, for example 32 A MCB or 2.5 mm². A short term such as cable or tape often works best.

### How do I browse by category?
Use the category bar at the top of the page. It lists the categories from the incoming supply to the loads: wires and cables, MCBs and circuit protection, modular boards, switches and sockets, LED lighting, insulation and accessories, electrical tools, and testers and measuring tools.

### Can I filter by brand, price or availability?
Yes. In the shop, filter by category and brand, set a minimum or maximum price, and turn on In stock only. Active filters show as chips you can remove one by one.

### Can I sort products by price?
Yes. Choose a sort option in the shop: price low to high, price high to low, or name. Your filters stay in place.

### Why does my search show nothing?
Try a shorter or simpler term, or remove a filter. A brand, price or category filter can hide products that otherwise match.

### Where can I read a product's specifications?
Open the product page. The specification list shows ratings, sizes, pack contents and other figures for that part.

### What are product options?
Products of the same make that differ only in size or rating, such as a wire in several cross-sections or a box in several module counts, are grouped on one product page. Choose an option and its price and stock update with it.

### How can I compare two products?
Open both product pages and compare the specifications, price, brand and stock. Check the units for size and pack quantity. You can also ask the assistant to compare two ratings.

### Which currency does Power Pick use?
Prices, totals and receipts are in Indian rupees (₹).

## Stock and availability

### How do I check whether something is in stock?
The stock gauge on each product shows In stock, Low, Last few or Out of stock, with the number left when it is low. You can also ask the assistant about a product by name.

### What do In stock, Low, Last few and Out of stock mean?
In stock means plenty is available. Low and Last few show the exact number left. Out of stock means none can be ordered right now.

### Does adding an item to my cart reserve it?
No. Another order or a stock update can change availability before you check out. Items are held for you from the moment you place an order.

### Can I buy an out-of-stock item?
No. An out-of-stock item cannot be added to the cart or ordered. Choose another option, or check the listing again later.

### Is there a restock date?
Power Pick shows current stock only. It does not publish restock dates.

### Is stock checked again at checkout?
Yes. Price and stock are checked again when you check out. If an item sold out or the quantity is too high, you are sent back to your cart to fix it and the order is not placed.

## Choosing electrical products

### What should I compare when choosing a wire or cable?
Compare the cross-section in mm² (the size, such as 1.5 mm² or 2.5 mm²), the conductor material, the coil length and the colour. The cross-section sets how much current the wire is meant to carry, so match it to the load.

### Which wire size and MCB suit a lighting circuit?
Home lighting circuits are commonly wired in 1.5 mm² copper with a 6 A or 10 A MCB. Treat that as a starting point and confirm the final choice with a licensed electrician.

### Which wire size and MCB suit general power sockets?
General power points are commonly wired in 2.5 mm² copper with a 16 A MCB. Check the appliance's rated current, and have the circuit planned by a licensed electrician.

### What suits a geyser, air conditioner or other heavy load?
Heavy loads usually need their own circuit, with wire and MCB chosen from the appliance's rated current. Larger wire sizes and 16 A or 32 A MCBs are common. Follow the appliance label and use a licensed electrician.

### How do I choose an MCB?
Look at the rated current in amps, the number of poles, the trip curve and the mounting. The MCB rating must suit the wire it protects. The listings show these figures, for example Rated current: 16 A, Poles: 1, Trip curve: C, Mount: DIN rail.

### What does the pole count on an MCB mean?
Poles are the number of lines the breaker switches. The listing shows the pole count, for example Poles: 1 for a single pole MCB.

### What do trip curves B and C mean?
The trip curve describes how quickly the breaker reacts to a surge. The listing shows the curve for each MCB. Ask a licensed electrician which suits your load.

### How do I choose a modular switch?
Check the current rating in amps, whether it is one way or two way, the format in modules, and the series. Switches and boxes of the same series, such as Plane M, are made to go together.

### What should I check on a socket?
Check the current rating, the pin pattern, the size in modules and the series. The listing shows each of these.

### How do I choose a modular box?
Match the module count to the switches and sockets you want to fit, and check the mount type and colour. Options run from 2 to 8 modules.

### What should I compare when choosing an LED bulb?
Compare the power in watts, the base type, the light output in lumens and the colour temperature in kelvin. Make sure the base matches your holder.

### What do the colour temperatures on LED lights mean?
Lower values such as 3000 K give a warmer, yellowish light. Around 4000 K is neutral white. About 6500 K is a cooler daylight white.

### What should I know before buying an LED strip?
Check the length, the input voltage and the power per metre. A listing may say the power supply is excluded, in which case you need to buy one separately.

### What should I compare when choosing a multimeter?
Compare the display counts, whether the range is manual or automatic, and what it measures, such as AC or DC voltage, resistance and continuity. Some models add true RMS measurement or come as a kit with leads.

### Which pliers or screwdriver should I look for?
Check the length, the jaw or tip type and the grip. The combination pliers listed come in several lengths, and the screwdriver lists its tip size and shaft length.

### How do I compare rolls of insulation tape?
Compare the width in mm, the roll length in metres, the colour and the material.

### Can the assistant confirm that a product is safe for my installation?
No. It can compare the published specifications. A qualified electrician should confirm the requirements and the final choice for your installation.

## Cart and checkout

### How do I add an item to my cart?
Open the product page, choose an option and a quantity within the stock shown, and select Add to cart.

### How do I change a quantity in my cart?
Open your cart, change the quantity for the item and choose Update. Check the new total before you check out.

### How do I remove an item from my cart?
Choose Remove beside the item in your cart.

### Why can't I add or order the quantity I entered?
The quantity must be a whole number within the stock available. If your cart holds more than is in stock, the cart shows how many are available and offers to lower the quantity.

### Where do I see my cart total?
The order summary in your cart shows the subtotal and total. Checkout does not add a separate delivery or tax line.

### What are the steps at checkout?
Cart, then Delivery, then Payment. You review your items, confirm the delivery address and phone number, and then choose cash on delivery or online payment with Razorpay.

### Do I have to sign in to check out?
Yes. Your cart is kept while you browse, and the cart page shows Sign in to check out if you are not signed in.

### What delivery details are required?
A full name, a 10-digit Indian mobile number and an address (line 1, city, state and 6-digit PIN code) are required to place an order. Address line 2 is optional and delivery is within India only. They come from your saved profile when you have one, and you can change them at checkout.

### What happens when I place an order?
The items are held for you. With online payment the order is Pending and you are taken to the payment page; it is confirmed once the payment is verified. With cash on delivery the order is Confirmed straight away and you pay in cash on delivery.

### Why did my cart total change at checkout?
Price and stock are checked again at checkout. If a price changed, checkout shows the new total before anything is reserved.

### Why was an item removed from my cart?
If an item is no longer sold, it is removed from the cart and you see a message before you check out. Review the cart and continue.

### Can I order the same items again?
Yes. Open a past order and choose Order again. The items that are still sold and in stock are added to your cart at today's prices, and the page tells you what could not be added.

## Wishlist and reviews

### How do I save a product to my wishlist?
Use the Save to wishlist heart on a product page or product card. You need to be signed in, and if you are not, you are asked to sign in first.

### Where is my wishlist?
Open Wishlist from the header or from your account area. It shows the products you saved, with their current price and stock.

### Can I move a wishlist item to my cart?
Yes, when it is in stock. Moving it adds one to your cart and removes it from the wishlist. If it is out of stock, it stays on the wishlist.

### Who can write a review?
A signed-in customer who has a paid order for that product, once the order is Confirmed or later. Staff accounts cannot review products.

### How do I write or delete a review?
Open the product page and use the Reviews section. You can leave one review per product, with a rating from 1 to 5, and delete it to write a new one.

### Why can't I review a product?
Reviews are limited to customers with a paid order for that product. Sign in and make sure the order is paid and confirmed.

### Are reviews shared between options of the same product?
Yes. Options of the same product and brand, such as different sizes, share one set of reviews.

## Payments and receipts

### What payment methods does Power Pick accept?
You can pay online through Razorpay, using UPI, cards or netbanking in its secure checkout, or choose cash on delivery and pay in cash when the order arrives.

### How does Cash on Delivery work?
Choose Cash on delivery at checkout. The order is confirmed straight away, its items are held for you and nothing is charged online. You pay the order total in cash when it is delivered, and the order's payment stays Unpaid until staff record the cash as collected.

### Can I cancel a cash on delivery order?
Yes, while the order is Pending or Confirmed. Open it and choose Cancel order. Its items return to stock once. After staff start processing it, ask the store through the Contact page.

### Is it safe to pay on Power Pick?
Payment is taken in Razorpay's secure checkout, where you can pay with UPI, a card or netbanking, depending on what Razorpay offers for your payment. Power Pick never sees or stores your card or UPI details.

### How do I pay for an order?
For online payment, you reach the payment page after you place the order. Choose Pay, then complete the payment in Razorpay's secure checkout with UPI, a card or netbanking. For cash on delivery there is nothing to pay online; keep the order total ready in cash.

### Where do I enter my card or UPI details?
Only in Razorpay's secure checkout window. Power Pick never sees or stores them, and you should not type them into the assistant.

### What should I do if my payment fails?
Open the order and check its recorded payment status. If the order is still Pending and unpaid, use Retry payment on that order. You do not need to place a new order.

### What happens if I close the Razorpay window?
Closing the window does not show whether you paid. Open the order and check the payment status before paying again, because a payment can still be confirming.

### How do I check a payment?
On the payment page choose Check payment status, or open the order and look at its Payment section.

### How is a payment verified?
Power Pick checks the payment with Razorpay on the server before it marks the order as paid. A verified payment moves the order to Confirmed.

### What do the payment statuses mean?
Paid means the payment was verified, or, for cash on delivery, that staff recorded the cash as collected after delivery. Unpaid means no payment yet, which is normal for a cash on delivery order until it is delivered. Failed means the online payment attempt did not go through.

### What is a payment reference?
It is the identifier Razorpay gives a payment. Power Pick keeps it so a payment can be matched to its order.

### Where can I see my payment history?
Open Payment history from your account area. It lists the method, status and reference for each payment.

### How do I get a receipt?
Open the order and choose Print receipt. The receipt shows the order number, date, delivery address, items with prices, total and payment details, and you can print it from your browser.

### Does Power Pick add delivery charges or tax?
Checkout shows a single total from the item prices and quantities, with no separate delivery or tax line. The store has not published delivery charges.

## Orders and delivery

### Where can I find my orders?
Sign in and open Orders from your account area. Open an order to see its items, delivery address, payment and status.

### How do I track my order?
Open the order. It shows a status line from Pending to Delivered, with the current step marked. The assistant can also tell you the status of your latest order when you are signed in.

### What is the difference between order status and payment status?
Order status shows where the order is in fulfilment. Payment status records whether the payment was verified or, for cash on delivery, whether the cash was collected.

### What does Pending mean?
The order is placed and waiting for online payment. You can pay for it or cancel it from the order page.

### What does Confirmed mean?
Payment has been received and verified, or the order was placed with cash on delivery. Staff have not started preparing the order yet.

### What does Processing mean?
Staff are preparing the order.

### What does Shipped mean?
Staff have marked the order as shipped.

### What does Delivered mean?
Staff have marked the order as delivered. A cash on delivery order then becomes Paid once staff record the cash as collected.

### What does Cancelled mean?
The order will not go ahead. Its items were returned to stock. You can place the items in a new order with Order again.

### What does Payment review mean?
A payment arrived after the order had already been cancelled, so store staff will look at it. You do not need to pay again.

### Does tracking show a courier's live location?
No. Status is set by store staff, and the site is not connected to a courier's tracking system.

### When will my order arrive?
Power Pick has not published delivery times, and the order status does not include a delivery date.

### How do I cancel an order?
You can cancel your own order yourself while it is Pending and unpaid, or, for cash on delivery, while it is Pending or Confirmed. Open it and choose Cancel order. A paid order cannot be cancelled from your order page.

### What happens to stock when an order is cancelled?
When an unpaid or cash on delivery order is cancelled, its items return to stock once.

### Can the assistant see or change my orders?
When you are signed in it can show the status of your own orders. It cannot see anyone else's orders, and it cannot cancel an order, take a payment or change anything.

## Help and store policies

### How can I contact the store?
Open the Contact page. It shows the store's contact details when the owner has added them. Until then, use the assistant for product and store questions and your order page for order questions.

### What can the assistant help with?
Finding products, checking price and stock, comparing ratings, suggesting products for a job, and explaining ordering, payment, tracking and your own order status.

### What happens to the questions I type to the assistant?
Questions are sent to Groq, an outside service, to help work out an answer. Do not type passwords, card numbers or other private details. See the Privacy page.

### What if the assistant does not know the answer?
It says so instead of guessing. Try a more specific question, check the product's specifications, or use the Contact page.

### Does Power Pick promise a warranty?
The store has not published a warranty. Check the manufacturer's documentation for the exact product.

### Does Power Pick promise refunds or returns?
Power Pick has not published a refund, return or replacement policy, and the assistant cannot cancel an order or a payment. For a question about a specific order, open it from Orders or see the Contact page.

### What is Power Pick?
Power Pick is an online store for electrical supplies, for electricians who need the right rating and pack size and for homeowners replacing a switch, adding a socket or fitting a light. Every listing shows its price, specifications and stock.

### Who keeps the assistant's answers up to date?
Store staff manage the FAQs from the staff workspace. Product prices and stock come straight from the live catalogue.
